Jarvis

Jarvis is a private household assistant built and run by its owner for his own family. It answers text messages from two people, using the owner's own calendar, mail and household notes.

Jarvis is not a product and is not offered to the public. Nobody outside the household can sign in to it or use it.

What it does with Google

Jarvis signs in to one Google account, the owner's, so it can read that account's Gmail, save and send email drafts the owner has approved, and read and edit events on that account's Google Calendar.

See the privacy policy for how that data is handled.
